Biography

Bradley J Williams is a singer, actor, and musical theatre performer. A tenor with a strong foundation in both vocal performance and acting, Bradley enjoys performing everything from classic and legit musical theatre to contemporary and pop/rock material.

As a Performer

His performance experience includes roles such as Shrek in Shrek the Musical and Mr. Banks in Mary Poppins, along with extensive ensemble and concert work. His training and performance background have given him opportunities to explore both character-driven comedy and more dramatic, vocally demanding material.
