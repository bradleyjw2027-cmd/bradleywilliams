Biography

Bradley Williams is a singer, actor, and musical theatre performer graduating in May 2027. His recorded selections range from the golden-age charm of She Loves Me to contemporary scores including Big Fish, Anastasia, and Newsies — alongside Andrew Lloyd Webber's The Phantom of the Opera and Moses Hogan's celebrated arrangement of This Little Light of Mine.

That breadth is deliberate: Bradley is drawn to material that asks something different of the voice and the actor behind it, from legit lyricism to contemporary drive and choral tradition.

Artistic identity

Bradley approaches each song as a piece of storytelling first — whether standing in the Phantom's final lair, racing toward redemption in Big Fish, or lifting a spiritual in four-part harmony. His reel is built to show range with intention: every selection is a different room in the same house.
